FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

If something's not here, message us on Telegram — we reply within an hour during work days.

How long until I reach Band 7?

Most students with a starting band of 5.5–6.0 reach Band 7 in 8–12 weeks of focused study (about 6 hours/week). We send a personalised timeline after your free diagnostic.

Do I have to buy a full bundle?

No — pick individual modules. Many students only need Writing + Speaking. Bundles just save money if you need everything.

Is Writing & Speaking graded by AI or humans?

Humans, always. AI assists our teachers, but the final feedback and band you see is from a Band 8.5+ scorer.

What if I don't hit my target band?

Free study extension until you do — as long as you complete the assigned lessons and at least 2 mock exams.

Which exam — IELTS Academic or General?

Academic is for universities and most professional registrations. General is for migration and work routes. Not sure? Take our free 5-minute quiz.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes — Click, Payme, and Stripe all support 3-month instalments on bundles. No interest, no hidden fees.
