    The recommendation

    Why Standard.

    ↳ cart + discounts appear at Tariff 2

    The Technical Task is explicit. It lists a cart, live price calculation and discounts. Only Tariff 2 crosses that gate. Starter is a fine site, but it cannot sell a module.

    • Cart, price calculation and discounts — named in the Technical Task, covered only from Standard up.
    • The AI module is marked “to be discussed” in the brief. Premium prices it now; you decide it later.
    • Standard ships the full storefront in ~8 weeks. AI can follow as a phase two, on the same codebase.
    Not sure about AI?

    Take Standard. Then sit one scoping call with us. We map the AI module against real student volume and inference costs. If the numbers work, Premium scope is added as phase two — nothing is rebuilt.
