FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

How long until I reach my target band?+

Most students who start at 5.5–6.0 need 8–12 weeks. Plan on about six hours a week. Your free diagnostic gives you a personal timeline.

Do I have to buy a bundle?+

No. Pick single modules. Many students only need Writing and Speaking. A bundle only saves money when you need most modules.

Who marks my Writing and Speaking?+

People, always. Every marker holds Band 8.0 or above. Feedback returns within 24 hours, against the same rubric examiners use.

What if I miss my target band?+

You retake the module free. The condition: finish your lessons and sit at least two mocks. 92% reach their target without needing the retake.

Academic or General — which do I need?+

Academic is for universities and professional registration. General is for migration and work routes. Unsure? Ask at the free diagnostic.

Can I pay in instalments?+

Yes. Click, Payme, and Stripe support three-month instalments on bundles. No interest, no hidden fees.

Will there be AI practice?+

AI practice tools — in discussion for a future issue. Today, people mark every script and every interview. Business English arrives first; it is coming soon.
